# Ground traffic and the runway watch rules

Loaded when Claude reads matching code. Background: docs/taxi-guidance.md. Full text of each rule: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md.

- [TRF-1] `GroundTrafficSuppression` (pure, not a MainForm lambda) silences Caution/Warning on the takeoff roll, with no route and in a STILL-ROLLING rollout; the 120° arc never gates Awareness, Alt+G stays ungated; a fast landing exit filters speech, never mutes the monitor (more: see full). Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-1
- [TRF-2] The queue position counts only the CONTIGUOUS line (`GroundTrafficLogic.QueueAheadOf`, 250 m gap), worded by `ReadQueue` alone: "departure queue" only on a takeoff-runway route with its head within 150 m of the route end; a queued aircraft stays queued while it moves up, and a line once at the hold stays the departure queue (more: see full). Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-2
- [TRF-3] The runway watch never says "clear", only "no traffic seen"; it is keyed by `RunwayWatch.Key` (its reason's `IdentityKey`, else the sorted runway keys) so hold to takeoff wait is ONE watch, its first status is ALWAYS spoken, runway events queue while `Holding`/`Vacating`, and occupant and final records stay separate (more: see full). Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-3
- [TRF-4] Ground-traffic callouts go through `TrafficSpeechPolicy.Plan` (Alt+G's summary speaks directly, [TRF-1]): only Warning, RunwayCritical and Caution interrupt, only a STRICTLY more urgent one cuts another within 3 s, a withheld interrupt is never latched or queued, latches commit in `TrafficCallout.OnEmitted` (more: see full). Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-4
- [TRF-5] "... ahead is moving." comes from a LATCHED departure (`QueueMovementPolicy.Step`), nearest aircraft only; "Move up" (`EvaluateNudge`) speaks only on a joined route off runway pavement, below 1 kt, nothing else within a 50 ft gap, at most 3 times (more: see full). Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-5
- [TRF-6] The held runway the watch scopes and the HoldShort status readout speaks come from the ONE `Navigation.HeldRunwayLabel.Resolve` (`GetGroundTrafficContext`, `GetStatusAnnouncement`), never a mirrored field: PR #247's `_heldRunwayLabel` was never assigned, so no hold-short was watched. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-6
- [TRF-7] A runway CROSSING is ONE watch: a single-runway watch whose sources all end lingers (`ApplyLinger`, `RunwayWatchLinger`: same key, Holding mode; multi-runway watches never linger) until clear on the far side, 30 m back out on its own side, or 60 s; never restore the stop-and-restart that spoke a second full status mid-crossing. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-7
- [TRF-8] The runway-watch gate closing SUSPENDS the watch (`SuspendWatch`), never ends it: the same key back within `RunwayWatchScopes.WatchResumeGraceMs` (15 s) resumes it with no new first status, re-arming only `_watchStartedUtc` to the resume; another watch adopted, an airport or database change, or the grace lapsing ends it. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-8
- [TRF-9] Known runway traffic is recorded by `WatchedRunway.Key`, never the spoken designator; `EvaluateRunwayWatch` purges an id whose runway left the scan (`IdsOutOfScope`) SILENTLY, only AFTER the scan loop has refreshed every seen id's key and before `ForgetAbsent`, and a purged id never feeds `EmptiedRunwayKeys`. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-9
- [TRF-10] The first-status re-arm (`ShouldRearmOnModeChange`) fires ONCE per watch, critical-only; from Holding its 10 s window runs to the hold's RELEASE (`_holdReleasedUtc`, set as a crossing linger begins, dropped once a watch is adopted with no linger), never to the change to OnRunway; a re-armed status completed silently marks nothing known. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-10
- [TRF-11] Every `GroundTrafficMonitor` rule reads the injected `_utcNow`, never `DateTime.UtcNow`, or the headless harness's simulated seconds hide defects; its fake sweep answers only after `RequestGroundTrafficData` returns. A completion counts only under the outstanding `_sweepRequestId`, and at most one sweep is outstanding. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-11
- [TRF-12] Every spoken aircraft distance and every zone is the GAP between two `AircraftHull` outlines (`GroundTrafficLogic.HullGapM`), never a centre distance; a hull is the installed model's (`GroundTrafficModelCatalog`), else its type's size, else its reported span scaled, else `AircraftHull.Default`, the widebody. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-12
- [TRF-13] The own aircraft's direction of travel is `GroundTrafficMonitor.OwnDirection` (its track when it moves tail-first), never the nose heading alone: the closing speeds, the closest approach, the `ForwardArcDeg` arc of "Slow down" and "Stop" and the fast poll follow it, and pushed back there is no route to judge by; speech stays nose-relative. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-13
- [TRF-14] Every "Stop" is answered once: by "Clear of ..." (`GroundTrafficLogic.IsClearOf`, `TrackedGroundAircraft.StopSpokenUtc`; also once it has gone) or, for the aircraft dead ahead in a line, by "... ahead is moving" alone; never "Clear of" for a stopped aircraft still in the arc of travel, however far back the pilot stopped. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-14
- [TRF-15] The zone lead accounts for the traffic's own motion: closing on the pilot (`TrafficView.ItsClosingMps`, a near closest approach, not `PassesClear`) adds `ZONE_LEAD_SEC` of ITS closing speed; moving away (`ItsRecedingKts`, the aircraft followed) makes it the following distance (`ZoneLeadFt` with `recedingKts`). Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-15
- [TRF-16] A traffic aircraft is named through `GroundTrafficLogic.NamingType` and `SpokenAirline` alone: the type its title names, else the designator its package declares (`GroundTrafficModelCatalog.Declared`), said by `SpokenTypes` (else read as its code, never its maker); the airline its model gives, else the one installed liveries name. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-16
- [TRF-17] What is IN THE WAY, and "very close" beside the path, is `GroundTrafficLogic.PassClearanceM` (two thirds of the ICAO stand clearance: 5, 3 or 2 m) against the strip the own wings sweep (`RouteClearanceM`), never a band round the route or a fixed gap; moving traffic that `PassesClear` (the parallel taxiway) is no threat. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-17
- [TRF-18] The one aircraft the pilot asked to be told about (`GroundTrafficMonitor.WatchUntilPassed`) speaks only through `EvaluateWatched` (`TrafficCalloutKind.Watched`, state committed in `OnEmitted`), never an Awareness or "on your route" line; "Clear of ...:" only once `GroundTrafficLogic.HasPassed`, never for one that has stopped. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-18
- [TRF-19] With taxi guidance not engaged (`GroundTrafficSuppression.IdleOnly`, `IdleCheck`) the proximity gate opens ONLY for a pushback (`PushbackInProgress`) or a watched aircraft, speaking only `TrafficSpeechPolicy.SpeaksWhileIdle` lines; the takeoff roll and the landing rollout yield to nothing and end a watch. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-19
- [TRF-20] An aircraft standing on a stand (`StandSupplier`, `GroundTrafficLogic.IsOnStand`, `ParkedOnStand`) earns no Awareness line: it stays in the Alt+G summary and is "Slow down" and "Stop" as any other when in the way ([TRF-17]). Never silence Caution or Warning by where an aircraft is parked. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-20
- [TRF-21] `GroundTrafficAircraftSizes.Agrees` refuses a measured outline only when over 15% SMALLER than its type's published size, or half as large again: never for being larger (the model as drawn, the safe side). The size table's helicopter rows are overall lengths, rotor turning. Full: docs/invariants/ground-traffic.md#trf-21
